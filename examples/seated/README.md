# Seats from the author's game

Props from the game SpriteCast was made for, each with the rain girl seated on it by `seat.py` (`seat/rain_girl-<facing>/`:
front.png, seat.json, preview.png): a dining chair seen from the front and the back, a church pew from behind, and a curved park bench
(built from `"arc"` blocks) from the front and the back. The dining chair and the pew were made from 3D models that are not included
(too big) - their renders and heights are, so `seat.py` runs on them as they are. The benches' blocks are in their specs.
