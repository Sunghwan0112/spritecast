how

White left over: magic erase, click it. alike: how near a colour counts as the same; whole frame: every pixel of it, not only the patch that touches (mind an eye's white, a snowball).
A face: select a good one, copy, go to another frame, paste, click to set it down - or paste in every frame. move drags what is selected; the arrow keys nudge it a pixel.
Change colour: click a colour in the frame - it becomes the chosen colour.
3D render beside (3) or faint under: what the frame was drawn from, in the same place - to tell paper from a real gap.
Wheel zooms, Space + drag (or the middle button) moves the picture, Alt-click picks a colour, Enter plays, ← → step frames. Red bar: marked by the review. Green: edited.
